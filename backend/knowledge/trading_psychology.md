## PART 1: CORE PRINCIPLES OF TRADING PSYCHOLOGY

### 1.1 The Performance Mindset

Trading is a performance activity, like athletics or surgery. Excellence requires:
- **Self-awareness**: Recognizing when you're in peak state vs. impaired state
- **Process focus**: Evaluating decisions by quality of process, not outcome
- **Deliberate practice**: Reviewing trades to identify patterns in performance, not just P&L
- **Emotional regulation**: Managing internal states that distort risk perception

The best traders do not eliminate emotion — they use emotion as *information* (market feel) rather than as a decision driver.

### 1.2 The Emotional States Affecting Trade Quality

#### A. PEAK STATE ("The Zone")
**Characteristics:**
- Calm, focused attention
- High pattern recognition
- Comfortable with uncertainty
- Rules feel natural, not forced
- Losses processed as information, not threats

**Market Conditions that induce peak state:**
- Familiar setups in familiar market regimes
- Well-rested, physically prepared trader
- Clear trading plan with defined scenarios

#### B. PRESSED STATE (Overtrading from Frustration)
**Characteristics:**
- Urge to "make back" losses
- Increasing position size after losses
- Entering low-quality setups
- Shortened analysis process
- Rationalizing rule violations

**Steenbarger's term:** "Pressing" — The trader is squeezing the trade process, forcing entries that aren't there.

**Trigger events:**
- String of consecutive losses (3+ in a row)
- Missed a big move (fear of missing out)
- External stress bleeding into trading
- PnL focus rather than setup focus

**Fibrios Detection Signal:** Look for high-volatility breakout moves that trap retail traders — these are often caused by institutional exploitation of pressed traders hitting stops.

#### C. PERFORMANCE ANXIETY STATE
**Characteristics:**
- Premature profit-taking (cutting winners short)
- Inability to hold positions through normal retracements
- Hesitation entering even high-quality setups
- Second-guessing confirmed signals

**Root cause:** Treating each trade as a referendum on trader identity rather than a sample from a probability distribution.

**Fibrios Detection Signal:** After a sharp directional move, look for weak pullback volume indicating hesitant, anxious longs/shorts entering — prime area for institutional reversal.

#### D. FEAR STATE
**Characteristics:**
- Refusing valid setups after a loss
- Reducing position size to meaningless levels
- Exiting at first sign of adverse movement
- Paralysis — watching without acting

**Fibrios Implication:** Fear-driven markets create exaggerated wicks and false breakdowns below support. These are liquidity sweeps targeting fear stops.

#### E. GREED / EUPHORIA STATE
**Characteristics:**
- Ignoring stop levels
- Adding to losing positions ("averaging down")
- Overexposure — too many correlated positions
- Feeling invincible after a winning streak

**Fibrios Detection Signal:** After extended trending moves (5+ waves in Elliott terms), euphoria participants create the exhaustion candles and volume climaxes that signal wave 5 completion.

## PART 2: COGNITIVE BIASES IN TRADING

### 2.1 Confirmation Bias
**Definition:** Seeking information that confirms existing position bias; ignoring contradicting evidence.

**In trading context:**
- A trader long XAUUSD ignores bearish CHoCH signals
- Analyst only recalls Elliott counts that support their direction
- News interpreted as bullish regardless of actual content

**Fibrios Countermeasure:** The multi-engine approach (Technical + Elliott + News) is designed to force contradictory inputs. A valid_setup requires alignment — if one engine disagrees, the confluence score drops automatically.

### 2.2 Loss Aversion (Prospect Theory)
**Definition:** Losses feel approximately 2x more painful than equivalent gains feel pleasurable. This creates asymmetric behavior:

- **Wins:** Cut too early (small gain feels like relief)
- **Losses:** Hold too long (avoiding the pain of realizing the loss)

**Result:** Small wins, large losses — the *opposite* of what a profitable system requires.

**Fibrios Countermeasure:** Pre-defined TP1/TP2 and SL in trade_plan enforce a mechanical exit process that overrides emotional impulse.

### 2.3 Anchoring Bias
**Definition:** Over-reliance on the first piece of information encountered (the "anchor").

**In trading context:**
- Trader bought at $2,400; refuses to accept bearish reversal because "it should go back to $2,400"
- News article mentioned $3,000 target; all subsequent analysis biased toward that level
- Previous day's high/low treated as immovable reference points

**Fibrios Countermeasure:** Each analysis session starts fresh. The system evaluates current market structure, not yesterday's expectation.

### 2.4 Recency Bias
**Definition:** Overweighting recent events relative to historical base rates.

**Manifestation:**
- After 5 bullish days, assuming the next will also be bullish
- After a failed setup, avoiding the same pattern even when statistically valid
- VIX spike causes permanent risk-off stance even after VIX normalizes

### 2.5 Gambler's Fallacy
**Definition:** Believing that after a string of losses, a win is "due."

**Dangerous manifestation in trading:**
- After 4 failed longs, doubling size on the 5th long because "the setup MUST work now"
- Each trade is statistically independent — the market has no memory of your losses

### 2.6 Overconfidence Bias
**Definition:** Overstating accuracy of own predictions; underestimating uncertainty.

**Peak performance period risk:** A winning streak inflates perceived edge. Traders add size, trade more frequently, and take lower-quality setups — exactly when regression to the mean is most likely.

## PART 3: MARKET EMOTIONAL STATE IDENTIFICATION

These are crowd psychology states observable in price action — distinct from individual trader psychology.

### 3.1 CAPITULATION
**Definition:** Forced selling by trapped longs who can no longer hold positions.

**Price action signature:**
- Sharp acceleration down (velocity increases, not just magnitude)
- Volume climax — highest volume in recent history on a down candle
- Wide-range down candle often closing near lows
- Immediately followed by reversal (buyers absorb all supply)

**Elliott context:** Typically marks end of Wave 3 extensions or Wave C completions
**ICT context:** Final liquidity sweep below a major swing low
**Fibrios signal:** CAPITULATION → HIGH PROBABILITY LONG SETUP

**Metrics to detect:**
- Volume > 3x 20-day average on a down candle
- Candle range > 2x ATR(14) on the downside
- RSI at or below 20

### 3.2 EUPHORIA / CLIMAX TOP
**Definition:** Panic buying by FOMO participants who fear missing further gains.

**Price action signature:**
- Steep acceleration up (parabolic move)
- Volume climax on an up candle
- Wide-range up candle, but may close off highs (shooting star)
- Immediate reversal or consolidation follows

**Elliott context:** Marks Wave 5 extensions or Wave B overshoot corrections
**ICT context:** Stop hunt above equal highs, buy-side liquidity sweep
**Fibrios signal:** EUPHORIA → HIGH PROBABILITY SHORT SETUP

**Metrics to detect:**
- Volume > 3x 20-day average on an up candle
- Candle range > 2x ATR(14) on the upside
- RSI at or above 80

### 3.3 COMPLACENCY / LOW VOLATILITY REGIME
**Definition:** Market participants are not stressed — they expect current trends to continue indefinitely.

**Price action signature:**
- Narrow ATR, contracting ranges
- Low volume
- Tight consolidation at all-time highs or after extended moves

**Risk:** Complacency regimes precede volatility explosions. Position sizing should be reduced (stop distances are smaller but volatility regime shifts are sudden).

**Fibrios signal:** COMPLACENCY → REDUCE POSITION SIZE; watch for volatility regime breakout

### 3.4 PANIC
**Definition:** Indiscriminate selling across all assets — correlations go to 1.

**Signature:**
- All assets fall simultaneously (Gold, Stocks, Crypto, Bonds all down)
- VIX spikes above 30
- Breadth indicators collapse
- No technical levels hold — support means nothing

**Fibrios signal:** PANIC → NO POSITION. Wait for stabilization. Panic moves are not tradeable with normal setups.

### 3.5 DISTRIBUTION (Smart Money Selling into Retail Buying)
**Definition:** Institutional participants quietly selling to retail buyers who are convinced the trend continues.

**Price action signature:**
- Price makes new highs but volume is declining
- Repeated tests of same high level with diminishing momentum
- Absorption pattern: rallies fade quickly, dips are shallow but not recovered
- RSI divergence: price higher, RSI lower

**Elliott context:** Wave 5 with RSI/MACD divergence
**Wyckoff context:** Distribution Phase B/C (see Wyckoff file)
**Fibrios signal:** DISTRIBUTION → Look for SELL setups on rallies

### 3.6 ACCUMULATION (Smart Money Buying into Retail Selling)
**Definition:** Institutional participants quietly buying from retail sellers who are convinced the trend continues down.

**Price action signature:**
- Price makes new lows but volume declining
- Wicks below support levels (stop hunts) that immediately recover
- Repeated tests of same low without continuation
- RSI divergence: price lower, RSI higher

**Elliott context:** Wave A/B of corrective pattern — setting up for larger Wave 1 or new impulse
**Wyckoff context:** Accumulation Phase C (Spring) — see Wyckoff file
**Fibrios signal:** ACCUMULATION → Look for LONG setups on dips

## PART 4: PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION FRAMEWORK (Steenbarger's 101 Lessons — Key Themes)

### 4.1 Journal-Based Pattern Recognition (Lessons 1-10)
The trading journal is the primary self-improvement tool. It captures:
- **Entry context:** What was the setup, what was the market state
- **Internal state:** Were you calm, anxious, pressed?
- **Decision quality:** Was the entry rule-based or impulsive?
- **Outcome analysis:** Did the loss/win result from good or bad process?

The goal: identify patterns in *decision quality* that correlate with *outcome quality*.

### 4.2 Emotional Recognition Skills (Lessons 11-25)
Key principle: **"Name it to tame it"** — labeling an emotion reduces its intensity.

Before every trade, brief internal check:
1. Am I calm? (Is my breathing normal? Is my body relaxed?)
2. Am I following my rules or reacting to the market?
3. Have I had any significant losses today that might be clouding judgment?

### 4.3 Solution Focus vs. Problem Focus (Lessons 26-40)
Problem focus: "Why do I keep losing?" → spirals into self-criticism
Solution focus: "When I trade well, what am I doing differently?" → identifies replicable behaviors

For the Fibrios system: if the system is generating losses, the solution focus is: "Under what market conditions do our signals perform best?" → feeds back into system improvement.

### 4.4 Building on Strengths (Lessons 41-55)
Elite performers specialize. They know:
- Which setups they execute best
- Which market regimes (trending, ranging, volatile) suit their style
- Which sessions (NY, London, Asia) have their highest edge

The Fibrios system supports this with: different models and knowledge bases per engine, specialized prompts per asset class.

### 4.5 Cognitive Restructuring (Lessons 56-70)
Replace performance-impairing thoughts with performance-supporting thoughts:

| Impairing Thought | Restructured Thought |
|------------------|---------------------|
| "I can't afford to lose this trade" | "This is one trade in a distribution of 1000 trades" |
| "I missed the move — I have to chase it" | "There will always be another setup" |
| "I knew I should have exited earlier" | "My rules said hold; I followed my rules; outcome is separate from process" |
| "The market is against me" | "The market is a neutral mechanism; I am responsible for my positioning" |

### 4.6 Deliberate Practice (Lessons 71-85)
Three conditions for improvement:
1. **Clear feedback:** Know immediately whether your decision was correct by your rules
2. **Specific error identification:** Not "I'm a bad trader" but "I entered before confirmation on 3 of 5 losses this week"
3. **Focused repetition:** Re-review identical setups to build pattern recognition without emotional contamination

### 4.7 Risk as Information (Lessons 86-101)
The final lesson cluster: the best traders *lean into* risk when conditions are right, rather than avoiding it universally.

Risk aversion is appropriate when:
- Market state is unclear (NEUTRAL bias)
- Multiple engines disagree (low confluence score)
- Emotional state is impaired (pressed, fearful)

Risk acceptance is appropriate when:
- High confidence setup (≥80 score)
- All engines aligned (strong confluence)
- Calm, process-focused internal state
- Clear invalidation level defined

## PART 5: MARKET STATE SCORING FOR FIBRIOS

### 5.1 Psychological Market State Score (0-100)
Use the following indicators to score the psychological state of the market:

| Market Condition | Score Contribution | Signal |
|-----------------|-------------------|--------|
| VIX < 15 | +20 | Low fear, complacent market |
| VIX 15-25 | +10 | Normal market state |
| VIX 25-35 | -10 | Elevated fear |
| VIX > 35 | -30 | Panic — avoid new positions |
| Volume < 0.7x average | -10 | Disengaged market |
| Volume 0.7x-1.3x average | 0 | Normal engagement |
| Volume > 2x average | +20 | Commitment/conviction move |
| Volume > 3x average on reversal candle | +30 | Capitulation/climax — high-probability reversal |
| RSI < 25 (oversold) | +15 | Capitulation exhaustion |
| RSI > 75 (overbought) | -15 | Euphoria exhaustion |
| Candle > 2x ATR | +10 | Impulse commitment |
| Candle > 3x ATR | +20 | Climax/exhaustion reversal |
| Price at multi-week high/low | -10 | Trend exhaustion risk |

### 5.2 Market State → Trade Bias
| Score | Market State | Fibrios Action |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| 80-100 | Optimal — high conviction | Full position size |
| 60-79 | Good — moderate conviction | Normal position size |
| 40-59 | Cautious — mixed signals | Reduce size 50% |
| 20-39 | Poor — elevated risk | Wait for clarity |
| 0-19 | Panic/Euphoria — extreme | NO POSITION |
